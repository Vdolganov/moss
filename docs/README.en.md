## History and working references

[PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) and [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md) contain the Russian product/design requirements and decision history. Older ARM plans, MOSS LAB documents, early scenarios, diagrams and review records remain in their original language as a development archive. They do not replace the current user guide; their former limits and gestures may differ from 0.9.0.

Documentation translations are maintained on `main`. The `v0.5.2` tag and original installation archives identify the released build. The English guide is provided separately in the [release](https://github.com/Vdolganov/moss/releases/tag/v0.5.2), without replacing application binaries.
