## English

All thirteen browser audit findings have corresponding fixes and regression coverage. Undo now follows accepted edits, save success waits for storage, and TAPE drafts persist independently of prepared assets. Sample-kit saving and assignment are explicit, atomic operations. Single-hit clearing preserves other lanes and shared automation. Individual lock SET/CLEAR/RESET actions are available through a parameter menu or face key + MENU; values keep their real units.

Navigation follows visible tabs, preserves track/effect positions and returns through one folder level at a time. Context menus use one registry, retain recovery actions and explain disabled entries. Footer buttons perform their labels. Empty states, track/scope labels, menu counters, fine/coarse hints, explicit desktop name typing, modal isolation and readable narrow-window scaling are implemented.

The monochrome design and existing instrument portraits remain intact. This is an interface-only browser update; native deployment, physical-button ergonomics and screen-reader certification are outside the completed checks. See `tests/ux-reliability.mjs` and the evidence directory above.
