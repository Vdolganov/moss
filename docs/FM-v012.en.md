# FM 0.12 — elastic percussion and sustained tones

[Русский](FM-v012.md)

FM is an original four-operator MOSS voice for kicks, noisy percussion, bells, elastic bass and wide sustained sounds. It is not a Model:Cycles, DFAM or DX7 emulator; old patches are not promised to sound identical.

## Engine and controls

Amplitude, modulation colour and pitch now have independent envelopes. PAIRS connects two carrier/modulator pairs; STACK feeds an intermediate pair into the next stage, retaining an intermediate stereo tap. Smoothed feedback, a centred sine component, direct body noise and a short noise excitation supply different kinds of weight and texture.

DYNAMICS connects velocity to modulation depth, pitch-punch amount and decay duration. The same pattern can therefore change character through accents instead of only changing volume.

L1/R1 cycles five VOICE banks. The physical columns remain Y / B / A / X. Hold a face button with left/right for fine edits or up/down for coarse edits. Tap A to audition; L1+R1 opens modes/presets.

| Bank | Y | B | A | X |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CORE | INDEX | RATIO | FEEDBACK | DECAY |
| BODY | DETUNE | NOISE | ATTACK | BODY |
| HIT | PUNCH | SWEEP | CLICK | DYNAMICS |
| COLOR | MOD DECAY | MOD HOLD | TONE | SPREAD |
| MOTION | RATE | MOTION | RELEASE | GLIDE |

DECAY approaches the held amplitude BODY. MOD DECAY independently approaches MOD HOLD. RELEASE follows note LENGTH expiry. RATE spans 0.01–20 Hz; MOTION moves index and detuning. DETUNE is a 0–100 macro, reaching 35 cents of nominal pair detuning. SPREAD controls stereo separation. TONE controls the top end without an intentional bass cut. PUNCH spans ±48 semitones, SWEEP 5–3000 ms; RATIO offers 16 harmonic/inharmonic choices from 0.5:1 through 16:1.

All twenty parameters appear in GRID → VOICE LOCKS. L1+arrows selects its nested bank; ordinary L1/R1 retains the outer GRID parameter tabs. LOCK ONLY updates a held voice without restarting its phase or envelopes; RESET restores the base value. Changing an envelope rate does not create a new excitation; use a note for another strike.

## Presets and demo

GLASS, BELL, HAZE, METAL, THUMP, SNAP, RUBBER and CLOUD cover glassy plucks, resonant metal, bass/percussion and long stereo pads. The preset picker scrolls through five visible rows and shows the selected position.

**ELASTIC MATTER** is a four-FM, 106 BPM sequence: THUMP / SNAP / RUBBER / CLOUD. Bass locks alter pitch punch, ratio and feedback. Pad lock-only steps move index, modulation hold and tone without retriggering. `native/prototype/tests/fm_render.cpp` creates eight dry examples, a 36-second demo WAV and a sample-free `.moss` sequence.

## Live portrait

Two internal modulator/carrier phase portraits follow the actual DSP samples. Amplitude scales them; spread separates them. AMP / MOD / BEND indicators show envelope state, with BEND including velocity/DYNAMICS. The live ratio caption reflects active locks; idle shows the base ratio. This is not a final output waveform: direct noise, TONE filtering and post-voice FX are outside the portrait. It contracts during release and rests after STOP.

## Research and licensing

[Model:Cycles](https://www.elektron.se/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Model-Cycles-User-Manual_ENG_OS1.13_241030.pdf) informed macro control and percussion/tonal range. [DFAM](https://cdn.inmusicbrands.com/Moog/DFAM/DFAM_Manual.pdf) informed velocity-sensitive excitation and independent envelopes. [Surge XT FM2/FM3](https://surge-synthesizer.github.io/manual-xt/index.html#fm2) informed musical ratios, inharmonicity and moving modulator relationships. Their code was not included.

The M-VAVE research inspected [Felucca](https://github.com/hugelton/Felucca) at `ca1db43a78455ae747766a2738f8580380d40bc1` and [JIANT](https://github.com/workeffortwaste/JIANT) at `ade4e8940f746dc4c7d183a3ab6dec06d8c28edb`. Felucca's FM6 is an Apache-2.0 msfa port using lookup tables and block-rate control; its whole firmware is GPL-3.0 and separate engines have other licences. No six-operator/DX7 engine was imported. JIANT was inspected as a derivative firmware with sound-driven visual state, not as a source of copied artwork or code.

[Fors Opal/Gem](https://fors.fm/opal) provided a visual reference for modulation versus amplitude; [Tracktion F.’em](https://www.tracktion.com/products/f-em) provided a broad FM capability reference. A full operator matrix would not fit this device's controls. MOSS implements its own compact voice and UI under the project's MIT licence; no proprietary firmware decompilation is included.

## Runtime, migration and verification

One FM voice per track, four tracks, up to two active track effects and two master effects. Fixed buffers, sine lookup, 2× rendering/FIR decimation, coefficient work every 32 samples. Sideband budgeting reduces aliasing but does not claim perfect bandlimiting at every feedback setting. Out-of-range carrier frequencies fade out. No allocation or file I/O in the audio callback.

MS18 stores all twenty FM parameters/locks. Older files retain their eight FM slots and receive migration defaults for added controls; presets become CUSTOM. Other instrument/sample assignments remain intact. Saving updates the format; older MOSS cannot read MS18.

Pitch/DC/bounds, sustained stereo, lock-only continuity, storage migration, controls and live telemetry are covered by tests. Short real-device load results are in the [verification report](verification/fm-v012/README.md). Digital measurements do not constitute a subjective headphone listening verdict.
